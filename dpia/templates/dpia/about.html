About this project

What this is

DPIA-Privacy-Impact-Assessment is a guided Data Protection Impact Assessment tool: a 5-step wizard walks through describing a proposed processing activity, judging its necessity and proportionality, flagging applicable risk factors, and documenting mitigations, ending in a calculated risk score, a recommendation, and an exportable PDF report.

Why it exists

This is Project 3 of a four-project portfolio built while transitioning from a legal career (data protection / legal team leadership in Brazil) into a Data Governance & Privacy Analyst role in the US. It's the forward-looking counterpart to Project 2 (Data-Mapping-ROPA): that project records processing activities already running, while this one assesses a new one before it launches — the same NimbusCart e-commerce scenario, seen from the other side of a launch decision. Project 1, LGPD-GDPR-CCPA-Comparative-Analysis, compares the underlying legal frameworks side by side.

How it works

  • A 5-step wizard collects the processing description, necessity/proportionality judgment, applicable risk factors, and documented mitigations, keeping state in the session until the final step is confirmed.
  • Each selected risk factor contributes a severity weight; each mitigation reduces its associated factor's contribution. The totals classify the assessment as Low, Medium, or High risk.
  • The risk level drives a recommendation (e.g. High → recommend legal/DPO consultation before launch), shown with a disclaimer on every screen it appears.
  • Every assessment can be exported as an audit-structured PDF at any time, and the dashboard lists all past assessments with status and risk level at a glance.

Built with

Django · SQLite (dev) · Django templates + vanilla CSS/JS · ReportLab (PDF) · Django's built-in test runner · session-backed multi-step form state

Built with Django to maintain a consistent stack across this portfolio's four projects, rather than picking a marginally leaner framework for any one of them.

Scope & disclaimer

All company, project, and scenario details in the sample data are fictional. The risk-scoring model and risk factor catalog are a simplified internal risk model inspired by common DPIA practice (loosely mirroring the shape of GDPR Art. 35 guidance and CPRA risk-assessment practice, in structure only) — this is not an official regulatory scoring system.

Every recommendation this tool produces, on screen or in an exported PDF, is a simulation for portfolio/demonstration purposes and does not constitute legal advice. Wherever a real legal citation, threshold, or deadline would normally belong, the source code carries a # TODO: VERIFY exact legal citation against official source comment instead of a stated-as-fact figure — see dpia/models.py and dpia/management/commands/seed_dpia.py.
